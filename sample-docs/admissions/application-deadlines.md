# Application deadlines

Sample content for the Box campus AI assistant. The university and dates are fictional.

| Application round | Deadline | Decision released |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early action | November 1 | Mid-December |
| Regular decision | January 15 | Late March |
| Transfer, fall entry | March 1 | Early May |
| Transfer, spring entry | October 15 | Late November |

Applications close at 11:59 PM Eastern Time on the deadline date. Late applications are reviewed only if space remains in the incoming class.

To confirm your spot, submit the enrollment deposit of $300 by May 1. Transfer students have 3 weeks from their decision date.
