# Admission requirements

Sample content for the Box campus AI assistant. The university and requirements are fictional.

## First-year applicants

- Completed online application and a $60 application fee. Fee waivers are available on request.
- Official high school transcript.
- One personal essay of up to 650 words.
- One letter of recommendation from a teacher or school counselor.
- Standardized test scores are optional.

## Transfer applicants

- At least 24 transferable credits with a minimum GPA of 2.75.
- Official transcripts from every college attended.
- A statement of up to 500 words explaining your reasons for transferring.

## International applicants

International applicants also submit proof of English proficiency, such as a TOEFL score of 90 or an IELTS score of 6.5, and a certified bank statement showing funds for the first year of study.
