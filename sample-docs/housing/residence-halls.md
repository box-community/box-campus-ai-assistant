# Residence halls

Sample content for the Box campus AI assistant. The university and policies are fictional.

| Hall | Room types | Who lives there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oak Hall | Double rooms, shared bathrooms | First-year students |
| Maple Hall | Single and double rooms, suite bathrooms | First-year and returning students |
| Cedar Apartments | 4-person apartments with kitchens | Returning and transfer students |

## Housing policies

- Quiet hours run from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM, Sunday through Thursday, and from midnight to 9:00 AM on Friday and Saturday.
- Guests can stay up to 3 nights in a row and must be registered at the front desk.
- Candles, space heaters, and halogen lamps are not allowed.
- Room change requests open 2 weeks after the semester starts.
