# Move-in dates

Sample content for the Box campus AI assistant. The university and dates are fictional.

| Group | Move-in date | Check-in hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-year students | August 20 | 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM |
| International students | August 18 | 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM |
| Returning students | August 22 | 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM |
| Spring semester | January 10 | 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM |

Book a 1-hour check-in slot in the housing portal at least 1 week before your move-in date. Bring a photo ID to pick up your room key at the residence hall front desk.

Early arrival before your move-in date costs $45 per night and requires approval from the housing office.
